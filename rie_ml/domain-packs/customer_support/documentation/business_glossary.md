# Customer Support Business Glossary

Domain pack: `customer_support_v0.1.0`  
Schema: [`schema/schema.json`](../schema/schema.json)

This glossary maps customer-support terminology to the schema used by the Customer Support domain pack.

---

## Ticket Metrics

### ticket_backlog
- **Definition:** Tickets that are still active in the support queue.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.status`, `tickets.is_internal`, `tickets.is_duplicate`
- **Related rules:** CS_R001, CS_CR001
- **Notes:** Internal tickets and duplicates are excluded from the active backlog.

### first_response_time
- **Definition:** Time from ticket creation to the first agent response.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.opened_at`, `tickets.first_response_at`, `departments.sla_response_minutes`
- **Related rules:** CS_R002
- **Notes:** Used for first-response SLA reporting.

### resolution_time
- **Definition:** Time from ticket creation to resolution or closure.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.opened_at`, `tickets.resolved_at`, `tickets.closed_at`
- **Related rules:** CS_R003
- **Notes:** Measured on closed or resolved tickets.

### escalated_ticket
- **Definition:** A ticket elevated to a higher urgency path.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.priority`, `tickets.status`, `departments.routing_priority`
- **Related rules:** CS_R002, future escalation rules
- **Notes:** High and urgent tickets are treated as priority work.

## Customer Feedback Metrics

### customer_satisfaction
- **Definition:** Customer satisfaction score captured after ticket handling.
- **Tables/columns:** `satisfaction_scores.score`, `satisfaction_scores.response_status`, `satisfaction_scores.submitted_at`
- **Related rules:** CS_R005
- **Notes:** Only received survey responses are counted.

### first_contact_resolution
- **Definition:** Ticket resolved without a reopen.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.reopen_count`, `tickets.status`
- **Related rules:** CS_R008
- **Notes:** A zero reopen count is treated as first-contact resolution.

### sla_breach
- **Definition:** Ticket that missed the department response or resolution target.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.sla_breach`, `departments.sla_response_minutes`, `departments.sla_resolution_minutes`
- **Related rules:** CS_R002, future SLA rules
- **Notes:** Stored as a boolean on the ticket record.

### support_tier
- **Definition:** Customer service plan tier.
- **Tables/columns:** `customers.support_tier`
- **Related rules:** future routing and prioritization rules
- **Notes:** Useful for later routing and escalation examples.

## Routing and Access

### agent_access
- **Definition:** Access control scope for support staff.
- **Tables/columns:** `agents.department_id`, `tickets.department_id`, `agents.role`
- **Related rules:** CS_R004, CS_CR003
- **Notes:** Agents should only see tickets within their own department by default.

### department_queue
- **Definition:** The operational queue owned by a support department.
- **Tables/columns:** `departments.department_id`, `departments.name`, `departments.queue_name`
- **Related rules:** CS_R009
- **Notes:** Active departments can receive tickets.

### customer_contact
- **Definition:** The customer requesting support.
- **Tables/columns:** `customers.customer_id`, `customers.email`, `customers.country_code`
- **Related rules:** CS_R001, CS_R005
- **Notes:** Used for routing, access, and satisfaction analysis.

### internal_ticket
- **Definition:** Ticket generated by an internal or sandbox account.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.is_internal`, `customers.is_internal`
- **Related rules:** CS_R001, CS_CR001
- **Notes:** Internal tickets are excluded from external KPI reporting.

## Lifecycle and Audit

### closed_ticket
- **Definition:** Ticket that is treated as final for reporting.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.status`, `tickets.closed_at`, `tickets.resolved_at`
- **Related rules:** CS_R003, CS_CR002
- **Notes:** `resolved` and `closed` are equivalent final states in the active pack.

### open_ticket
- **Definition:** Ticket that is still active but not yet closed.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.status`
- **Related rules:** CS_R003, CS_R001
- **Notes:** Includes open, pending, and waiting-on-customer states in backlog-style reporting.

### ticket_event
- **Definition:** Event record in the ticket history stream.
- **Tables/columns:** `ticket_events.ticket_id`, `ticket_events.agent_id`, `ticket_events.event_type`, `ticket_events.event_timestamp`
- **Related rules:** CS_R006
- **Notes:** Join ticket events by `ticket_id`.

### duplicate_ticket
- **Definition:** Ticket that duplicates another support case.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.duplicate_of_ticket_id`, `tickets.is_duplicate`
- **Related rules:** CS_R007, CS_CR004
- **Notes:** Duplicate tickets are excluded from backlog counts.

### duplicate_customer_email
- **Definition:** Multiple customer records sharing the same email address.
- **Tables/columns:** `customers.email`, `customers.customer_id`
- **Related rules:** future data-quality rules
- **Notes:** Flagged for review because it may indicate duplicate customer identity records.

### waiting_on_customer
- **Definition:** Ticket paused until the customer responds.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.status`
- **Related rules:** future backlog and aging rules
- **Notes:** A distinct lifecycle state for support processing.

## Support Operations

### high_priority_ticket
- **Definition:** Ticket with urgent operational importance.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.priority`, `departments.routing_priority`
- **Related rules:** future priority routing rules
- **Notes:** Often tied to enterprise support tiers.

### open_ticket
- **Definition:** Ticket that has not been resolved or closed.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.status`
- **Related rules:** CS_R001
- **Notes:** Includes open, pending, and waiting-on-customer states for backlog logic.

### agent_active
- **Definition:** Agent available to receive new assignments.
- **Tables/columns:** `agents.status`, `agents.is_active`, `agents.department_id`
- **Related rules:** CS_R004
- **Notes:** Inactive agents should not receive tickets.

### routed_ticket
- **Definition:** Ticket assigned to a responsible team or agent.
- **Tables/columns:** `tickets.department_id`, `tickets.assigned_agent_id`
- **Related rules:** CS_R004, CS_R009
- **Notes:** Routing is department-based first, then agent-based.
